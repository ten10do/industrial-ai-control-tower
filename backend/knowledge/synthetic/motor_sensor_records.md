# MOTOR-001 Sensor Validation Maintenance Records

Source classification: synthetic. These records are test fixtures, not manufacturer instructions.

## Vibration Sensor Intermittent Signal

Observed symptom: vibration values alternated between a fixed maximum and zero while motor load,
current, and speed stayed stable. Inspection found a loose connector at the accelerometer junction.
The technician isolated the motor under the site energy-control procedure, reseated the connector,
checked cable shielding, and compared the channel with a calibrated portable vibration instrument.

| Check | Observation | Recorded action |
|---|---|---|
| Connector continuity | intermittent | reseated and secured connector |
| Shield grounding | one end only | retained approved single-point termination |
| Reference instrument | stable reading | compared channel after reconnection |

## Bearing Temperature Channel Drift

Observed symptom: the bearing temperature channel increased while housing temperature measured by
a calibrated reference probe stayed steady. The technician checked RTD terminal resistance,
extension wiring, transmitter range, and calibration date. A corroded terminal caused added
resistance. The terminal was replaced and the measurement was verified against the reference probe.

## Current Sensor Plausibility Check

Observed symptom: one current channel reported an abrupt step without a corresponding change in
load, power, voltage, speed, or temperature. The technician verified current with a calibrated
clamp meter, inspected the current-transformer secondary wiring, and recorded the instrument serial
number and calibration status before returning the channel to service.

## Unsupported Fault Boundary

These synthetic records cover only vibration, temperature, and current measurement validation for
MOTOR-001. They do not support invented alarm codes, other equipment models, aircraft maintenance,
medical diagnosis, or autonomous equipment-control actions.
