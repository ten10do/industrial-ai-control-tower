# ADR-008: Human-in-the-Loop Safety Model

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Industrial actions can have physical and financial consequences. LLM-based recommendations are probabilistic and must not directly drive equipment.

## Decision

Require human approval for any action that can affect physical equipment, dispatch a work order, or override safety policy.

## Alternatives

- **Fully autonomous AI control**: Faster, but unacceptable for safety-critical industrial systems.
- **Rule-based automation without AI**: Safe, but cannot handle novel diagnostic scenarios.
- **Post-action review**: Allows action before human review, which is too late for hazardous operations.

## Consequences

- Latency increases for approved actions.
- The UI must clearly present evidence, diagnosis, and risk to approvers.
- All approvals and rejections are audited.
- Emergency stop or safety interlock systems remain independent of the platform.
