# ADR-007: MQTT / OPC UA Industrial Integration

## Status

Provisional — to be confirmed in Phase 1

## Context

Industrial equipment communicates through a variety of protocols. The platform must ingest telemetry and, in future phases, interact with control systems.

## Decision

Adopt MQTT for lightweight telemetry ingestion and OPC UA for structured industrial data access.

## Alternatives

- **Modbus**: Widely used, but lower level and less semantically rich than OPC UA.
- **Proprietary SCADA APIs**: May be required for specific deployments, but will be wrapped behind adapters rather than baked into the core.
- **HTTP webhooks from edge gateways**: Simple, but less efficient for high-frequency telemetry.

## Consequences

- MQTT enables pub/sub telemetry from many devices with minimal overhead.
- OPC UA provides structured data models for asset hierarchy and historical access.
- All control commands will pass through the safety and approval boundary; protocols are read-only by default until explicitly authorized.
